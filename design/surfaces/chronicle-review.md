# Surface brief: Chronicle review

<!-- impeccable:surface-schema 1 -->

**Mode:** Operate  
**Theme:** Light (study cockpit)  
**Routes:** `/c/[course]/[assessment]/play`, `/attempts/[id]`

## Success

The student reads the full question, selects answers with confidence, and understands Prep vs Exam rules before submitting.

## Layout

- Max width 1200 px centered.
- Question stage full width; prose ~70ch.
- Answers 2 col ≥768 px, 1 col below.
- Primary action after answers, never floating over tiles.

## Motion

- Spring selects on answers; reduced motion uses opacity only.
- Exam: no correctness motion or streak flame.

## Tokens

Use Chronicle table in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md). No Void dark tokens on this surface.
